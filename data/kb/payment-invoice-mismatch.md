---
title: Invoice amount, coupon or tax mismatch
category: payment
next_action: Compare checkout price log with invoice; issue corrected invoice and refund difference.
---
Policy: When the charged amount differs from the checkout price (coupon not applied, duplicate tax, wrong COD fee), Billing compares the checkout log with the invoice. Any overcharge is refunded to the source within 5-7 business days and a corrected invoice is emailed.
Customer message: We are reviewing the pricing on your invoice against your checkout details. Any amount charged in excess will be refunded to your original payment method and a corrected invoice will be emailed to you.
