title: Login, OTP, password or verification problems
category: account
next_action: Verify identity, reset credentials, check OTP delivery logs.
---
Policy: For login failures, missing OTPs, locked accounts or failing verification, verify the customer's identity via registered email, unlock the account, and send a reset link. Check SMS gateway logs for OTP failures. Never ask for the OTP itself.
Customer message: We have verified your account and sent a secure reset link to your registered email. If you still do not receive OTPs, please let us know your carrier and we will check our SMS delivery logs.
