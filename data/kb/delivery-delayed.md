---
title: Delayed or stuck shipment
category: delivery
next_action: Raise a trace request with courier partner; offer shipping fee refund if delay > 5 days.
---
Policy: For shipments past the promised date or stuck at a hub, Logistics raises a trace with the courier partner (response within 48 hours). If the delay exceeds 5 days past promise, the shipping fee (including express charges) is refunded. If the parcel is lost, the customer may choose a replacement or a full refund.
Customer message: We have raised a priority trace with our courier partner for your shipment and will update you within 48 hours. If it cannot be delivered, you can choose a replacement or a full refund.
