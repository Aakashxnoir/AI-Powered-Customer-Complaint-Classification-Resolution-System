Policy: Refunds to cards/UPI take 5-7 business days after approval; bank transfers up to 10 business days. Share the refund reference number (ARN) so the customer can check with the bank. If more than 10 business days have passed, escalate to Billing for a manual check. Wallet credits can be moved to the bank on request.
Customer message: Your refund has been initiated. Refunds usually reflect within 5-7 business days; we will share the bank reference number so you can track it. If it has been longer than 10 business days, we are escalating it for a manual check.
