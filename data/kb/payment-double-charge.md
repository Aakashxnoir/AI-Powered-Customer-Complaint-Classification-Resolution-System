---
title: Duplicate or failed payment debited
category: payment
next_action: Verify transaction IDs with payment gateway; trigger auto-reversal if duplicate.
---
Policy: If a customer is debited twice or debited for a failed payment, the duplicate/failed amount is auto-reversed by the payment gateway within 5-7 business days. Staff must verify the transaction reference in the gateway dashboard. If reversal is not visible after 7 business days, raise a manual refund with Billing.
Customer message: We have verified that the extra debit will be reversed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days. If it does not reflect by then, please reply to this message and we will raise a manual refund immediately.
