---
title: Defective or malfunctioning product
category: product
next_action: Approve return/replacement within 10-day window; else route to brand warranty service.
---
Policy: Products that are defective, stop working, do not charge or restart on their own are eligible for free replacement or refund within 10 days of delivery. After 10 days, the product is covered by manufacturer warranty and a service request is raised on the customer's behalf.
Customer message: We are sorry the product is not working as expected. If it is within 10 days of delivery we will arrange a free pickup and replacement or refund; otherwise we will register a warranty service request for you.
