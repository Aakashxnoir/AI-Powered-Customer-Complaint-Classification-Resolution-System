---
title: Product safety hazard (overheating, burning, shock)
category: product
next_action: Advise customer to stop use; escalate to Product Safety; quarantine batch if 3+ reports.
---
Policy: Any report of overheating, burning smell, smoke, sparks or electric shock is a safety issue. Advise the customer to stop using and unplug the product immediately. Escalate to Product Safety, offer a full refund with free pickup regardless of return window, and check for other reports of the same product.
Customer message: For your safety, please stop using the product and unplug it right away. We have escalated this to our Product Safety team and will arrange a free pickup with a full refund.
