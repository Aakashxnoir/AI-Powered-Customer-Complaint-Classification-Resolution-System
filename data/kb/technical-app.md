title: App/website errors, crashes and checkout failures
category: technical
next_action: Collect device/app version and screenshot; create engineering ticket; suggest workaround.
---
Policy: For crashes, errors, blank pages, slow app or broken features, collect device model, OS, app version and a screenshot. Suggest updating the app, clearing cache or using the website as a workaround. Create an engineering ticket; if checkout is blocked, offer to place the order on the customer's behalf.
Customer message: Sorry for the trouble with our app. Please update to the latest version or try clearing the app cache; meanwhile you can use our website. Our technical team is investigating, and if you are unable to check out we can help place the order for you.
