---
title: Unauthorised charge or account access
category: account
next_action: Freeze account, force password reset, escalate to Fraud & Security within 1 hour.
---
Policy: Any report of unauthorised charges or account access is treated as a security incident. Immediately lock the account, revoke active sessions, force a password reset, and escalate to Fraud & Security. Do not ask the customer to share OTPs or passwords. Unauthorised orders are cancelled and amounts refunded after investigation (up to 10 business days).
Customer message: For your safety we have temporarily secured your account and logged out all sessions. Our security team is investigating and will contact you shortly. Please never share your OTP or password with anyone, including our staff.
