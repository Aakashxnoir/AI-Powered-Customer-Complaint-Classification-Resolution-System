title: Marked delivered but not received / wrong address
category: delivery
next_action: Open a non-receipt investigation with courier (GPS + proof of delivery); resolve within 72 hours.

Policy: When an order is marked delivered but not received, or delivered to a wrong address, open a non-receipt investigation with the courier using GPS and proof-of-delivery. The investigation completes within 72 hours; if delivery cannot be proven, a replacement or full refund is issued.
Customer message: We have opened an investigation with our delivery partner using their proof-of-delivery records. We will get back to you within 72 hours, and if delivery cannot be confirmed we will send a replacement or issue a full refund.
