Complaint triage for support teams

ComplaintIQ reads each complaint, sets its category, sentiment and priority, routes it to a department, and drafts a reply from approved playbooks. Every decision shows its reason. A person approves every reply.

What it does

Category and sentiment

Sorts each complaint into one of six categories and scores its sentiment. The confidence for every class is shown.

Priority, with reasons

Fraud, safety, legal threats, urgency, amount and frustration add points. Each point is listed with its reason.

Routing

Each category maps to a department. Fraud and product-safety reports go to their own teams.

Duplicates

A complaint sent again by the same customer or for the same order is linked to the original. An agent confirms or rejects the match.

Spike alerts

The last 72 hours are compared with the 14 days before, by category, product and city. A sharp rise raises an alert.

Drafted replies AI

The two closest approved playbooks are retrieved. The reply is drafted from them and cites them. Staff approve, edit or reject it. Nothing is sent automatically.

Results on held-out test data

Test complaints come from templates the model never saw in training. Synthetic data flatters these scores, so the weakest result is listed too.

Questions

Does ComplaintIQ send replies on its own?

No. Every draft waits for an agent to approve, edit or reject it.

What was the classifier trained on?

The Bitext customer support dataset and synthetic complaints generated from 72 templates. Real complaints will likely score lower than the test set.

Can agents correct labels?

Yes. Category, sentiment, priority and department can each be corrected. The corrections feed retraining, which is kept only if held-out accuracy stays within 1 point.

Does it need a GPU?

No. It runs on CPU.

What if no Gemini key is set?

Drafts come from a template built on the matched playbook.

What are the limits?

English only. No authentication: the API is for local use behind the console proxy. The sentiment score is optimistic on this data. Full list in the documentation.
